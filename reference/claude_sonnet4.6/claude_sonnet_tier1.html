Our story

Made for the long run

Maison Toile started as a small atelier with one idea: clothes that look better the more you wear them. No trends, no throwaway pieces — just thoughtfully cut garments in fabrics that age well. We work with independent mills and keep our runs small so nothing ends up in a landfill.
